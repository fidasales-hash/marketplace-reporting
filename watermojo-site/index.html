Load-Shedding

Does it still work during load-shedding?

Most water backup systems store your water — but if the pump can't run, you still have no pressure. Standard systems need grid power to pressurise your home. During an Eskom outage, that means dry taps even with a full tank.

Every Watermojo installation can include an inverter-compatible pump and wiring. When load-shedding hits — stage 2 or stage 8 — your pump switches to inverter power automatically. No manual switchover. No dry taps.

Mention load-shedding when you enquire and we'll spec the right inverter connection for your property.
